# Hi, we are Smart Tech 👋

## 🚀 Smart Tech
### Technology that solves everyday problems

Smart Tech is a marketing and product-discovery project focused on finding common problems in daily life and identifying the most suitable technology item to solve them.

Our team studies people's needs, explores available technology products, compares possible solutions, and recommends the product that delivers the best combination of usefulness, quality, price, and convenience.

> 💡 Final Deliverable: A research-based technology recommendation concept that connects real customer problems with practical tech solutions.

---

## 📖 Project Overview

People face small technology-related problems every day, but they may not know which product can solve them or how to choose between similar options.

Smart Tech helps solve this problem by:

- Discovering real problems from everyday life
- Understanding the people affected by each problem
- Researching technology products that could provide a solution
- Comparing product features, prices, quality, and usability
- Recommending the most suitable item for the target customer

Our goal is to make technology choices easier, smarter, and more useful for everyone.

Smart Tech is designed for:

- People looking for practical technology solutions
- Customers who are unsure which tech product to buy

- Small businesses looking for affordable technology tools
- Anyone who wants technology to solve a real problem, not create a new one

---

## 🔍 Our Methodology

We follow a simple marketing and research process:

1. **Problem Discovery** — Find a real problem people experience in daily life.
2. **Audience Research** — Understand who has the problem and what they need.
3. **Market Research** — Study existing solutions, trends, and competitors.
4. **Product Search** — Find technology products that may solve the problem.
5. **Product Evaluation** — Compare features, price, quality, availability, and ease of use.
6. **Solution Selection** — Choose the product that best fits the audience and problem.
7. **Marketing Recommendation** — Explain the product's value and how to promote it.

---

## ✅ Product Evaluation Criteria

Each recommended product will be evaluated according to:

- Relevance to the problem
- Ease of use
- Product quality and reliability
- Price and value for money
- Availability
- Customer reviews and feedback
- Safety and privacy considerations
- Competitive advantage
- Suitability for the target audience
